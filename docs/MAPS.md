# Kalyeah maps (layouts)

Owner decisions on where things sit on each map. Rules for all maps are in `docs/SPEC.md` §6; sizes and exact positions
go into `unity/Data/*.json` when each map is blocked out and play-tested with bots.

## Barangay
Two halves: **Uptown** (cement: barangay hall, plaza, basketball court; open, long lines) and **Riverside** (market and
chapel, sari-sari row; tight streets). The centre is soil and a provincial river with homes of scrap wood and tin.

**Bases and jails** (jails sit to the side of the base, so a rescue is a flank, not a straight run, like Valorant's offset
spawns):
- **Uptown base:** the barangay hall steps. **Uptown jail:** the covered court beside the hall.
- **Riverside base:** the chapel yard. **Riverside jail:** the back lot of the sari-sari row.

**Routes:** the main path over the river bridge; the left alley through the riverside homes; the right route along the
creek bank; side connectors between them.

**Neutrals:**
- **Crocodile (dragon):** on a sandbar under the main bridge, in the middle: both teams fight over it.
- **Philippine Eagle (baron):** perched on the water tower on the creek-bank flank, fought from the ground below; taking it
  means leaving the main fight. The tower itself is not climbable.
- **Taho vendor:** on the left alley, just short of the river. **Fishball vendor:** on the creek bank, just short of the
  river. Both are equally far from each base.
- **Selecta bike (roaming buff):** rides a loop on the river road between the bridge and both flanks.

**Climb spots (high ground):**
- **Basketball court backboard stand** (Uptown): long lines over the court toward the bridge; open to fire from the plaza.
- **Sari-sari store roof** (Riverside): watches the tight streets; easy to rush from below.
- **Parked jeepney top** (near the main bridge): a mid-map spot for both teams.

**Side swap balance:** Uptown favours long range, Riverside close range; teams swap halves at half time, so each team plays
both.
