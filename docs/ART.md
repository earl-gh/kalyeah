# Art, UI and audio

Look: **Clash of Clans design language** (soft, chunky, painted-looking, toy-like) on a **League of Legends camera**, in a Filipino street. Inspired by, never copied (`docs/PROJECT.md` 3.8). The target is the owner's reference image; the work to get there is the visual overhaul in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## Where we are
The current art is a first pass built **in code**: procedural meshes and vector icons, with no asset files. It is soft-shaded and readable but not final. The plan replaces it piece by piece with real assets made in Blender (see "Asset pipeline").

### 3D (procedural, current)
- `scripts/view/art/low_poly.gd` (`LowPoly`): bevelled boxes, smooth cylinders and spheres, gable roofs, with vertex colours and **one shared matte material** (wrap lighting, soft rim, no hard speculars). Cartoon outlines come from an inverted shell (`add_outline`).
- `scripts/view/art/palette.gd` (`Palette`): the shared colour palette.
- `scripts/view/art/props.gd` (`Props`): sari-sari store, houses (one and two storey), laundry lines, tarpaulins, basketball ring, jeepney, market umbrella, the cover pieces (crates, bench, sawhorse, sign board), plants and trees, manholes, hopscotch, the thrown weapon objects, and the polymorph can. Meshes are cached and kept under a vertex budget by a test.
- **Cardboard walls** are the bahay-bahayan: disassembled boxes as single flat sheets (creases, tape, printing, curled flaps, a torn corner) leaning on sticks. Never stacked boxes.
- `scripts/view/street_map.gd` (`StreetMap`): asphalt, lane paint, a cross street with zebra crossings, curbs, sidewalks, house rows, base pads and posts, props. The lane geometry comes from `MapLayout` and never changes for looks.
- Lighting: one sun with **no visible shadows** (owner: the street, props, houses and kids show none; each kid keeps a soft blob under its feet; the shadow is fully see-through, not switched off, because switching the sun's shadow off made the street about 30% darker), 2x MSAA (no saturation grading and no haze any more; see "Light and colour by asset class").

### Characters (current)
- `scripts/view/art/kid_model.gd` (`KidModel`): all six kids are **real 3D models** in `assets/models/characters/<kid>.glb` (rigged GLB, ~9k triangles, one 1024 px baked texture), made light for phones by `tools/blender/build_kid_game_model.py` from the owner's full-resolution rigged kids (not stored in the repo). The skeleton uses `.L` / `.R` names (`hips`, `pelvis`, `spine.01…`, `chest`, `neck`, `head`, `shoulder`, `upper_arm` + twist, `forearm` + twist, `hand`, fingers, `thigh`, `shin`, `foot`, `toe`, spring bones for hair, skirt, pigtails and the belt bag) under a ground `root`; elbow and knee corrective blend shapes keep the joints from collapsing (the full models also have hip and shoulder ones, left out on phones because each blend shape is a full mesh copy). Clips (30 fps): `idle_alert`, `run`, `throw`, `throw_run`, `cast_one_hand`, `cast_run`, `scratch_head`, `trip`, `stunned`, `airborne_fall`, `airborne_fall_land`, `knockdown`; the game plays them under its own names (`idle`, `run`, `cast`, `spell`, `stun`, `airborne`, `land`, `ko`, `KidModel.CLIP_FOR`). In game the run cycle is paced by the ground speed (one cycle per 1.6 m) so the feet do not skate; a cast plays at the weapon's cast time, as a throw, or a one-hand spell for heals, shields and traps (`KidModel.is_spell`); a cast on the move plays `throw_run` / `cast_run` on the upper body only (a second, non-deterministic `AnimationPlayer`) so the legs keep running; airborne kids flail and land with a squash; a knockout plays the kid's own knock-down (they land on their back); the clips play in place (the sim owns positions). The stun stars are little 3D stars. **Junjun's own animation (owner):** his model (`CharacterDef.native_animation = true`) plays its own clips at their own speed: the run is not paced to the ground speed (and the footsteps follow his run cycle), a cast shows his throw or cast clip at normal speed for its whole length (not squeezed into the weapon's cast time), and a spell starts at the start of its clip. The other kids keep the game's pacing. `ModelLook` gives every model the matte look the owner approved: roughness 1, no metal, no normal map, wrapped diffuse, a soft rim, albedo x0.65. **Junjun** is the owner's rebuilt-rig boy (`Boy_rebuilt_rig.glb`, Mixamo skeleton, 10 clips): `build_kid_game_model.py` renames its bones and clips to the game's names, welds and decimates it to 14,000 triangles (the shorts and tank top overlap at the waist, and fewer triangles leave white blotches there) and bakes one 1024 px texture with a tight bake range (`KID_CAGE=0.002 KID_RAY=0.006`). He has no landing clip, so a landing plays nothing extra. Kids without their own model use Junjun's. No team colour on the kid itself (clothes stay the kid's own); the health bar's colour and number badge tell teams apart (no ring under the kids, owner). HUD cards use `assets/portraits/<kid>.png` (rendered and cropped by `tools/blender/render_portrait.py`).
- All six share one height and proportion; the hitbox is always `GameRules.player_radius`. Outfits, hair and skin tones (varied across the roster) come from `CharacterDef` (`data/characters/*.tres`). A ground ring in the health bar's colour shows the team (`KidModel.ring_mesh`, visual pass); there is no bandana or armband.
- Written with respect (PROJECT 3.2): identity through clothes and style only.

## HUD and UI
- **Skin and kit (UI rebuild, phase 1).** `UiSkin` (`scripts/ui/ui_skin.gd`) draws every button and panel in code: small anti-aliased textures rendered once from a rounded-rectangle distance field (gradient face, gloss, lit top edge, dark lip, outline, drop shadow in the padding), used as 9-slice style boxes. Button variants gold (the one main action), blue, green, red and slate; panel kinds glass, raised, inset and night. `KalyeahTheme.style_button` and `painted_panel` delegate to it, so every screen wears it. Kit: `ui_kit.gd` (page header, card, chip, setting row), `ui_tab_bar.gd`, `ui_segmented.gd`, `ui_toggle.gd`, `ui_profile_chip.gd`, `ui_backdrop.gd`. The owner's button art can replace the textures without touching a screen. Direction and critique: `docs/audits/2026-10-20-ui-rebuild-critique.md`.
- **Feedback and profile (round 7).** `UiToast` (`ui_toast.gd`) answers an action with a short pill above the tab bar (ok, info, warn, error; one at a time, its own canvas layer, never takes a touch, no rise or fade with Reduce motion). `UiStatusStrip` (`ui_status_strip.gd`) dresses a screen's status line: amber while waiting, red for a problem, blue for a note. `ProfileSheet` (`profile_sheet.gd`) is the name and face picker. The UI gallery page is gone; a test keeps every chip fill (`test_ui_round7.gd`) at 4.5:1 or better. Chip fills (`UiKit.CHIP_*`) all keep white text at 4.5:1 or better.
- **Code boxes and the walkthrough (round 8).** `UiCodeBoxes` (`ui_code_boxes.gd`) is the room code as eight boxes: an invisible LineEdit over them takes the keyboard and paste, the boxes show it, the next box is lit gold, a wrong code turns them red; read-only it shows the code in the room header. `CoachMarks` (`hud/coach_marks.gd`, shader `shaders/coach_spot.gdshader`) is the first-run walkthrough: a dim with round gold-ringed holes over the joystick and the weapon buttons and a card with Next and Skip. `HowToPlayPage` shows the same steps in Settings.
- **Skin speed (round 9).** Textures are drawn left half then mirrored (pixel-identical, about 2 to 3 times faster), and the ones the first screens use are built one a frame behind the boot screen's loading bar (`UiSkin.warm_next`, 20% of the bar), so no first tap pays for them. A test fails if a first screen draws a texture that is not on the warm list.
- Typography (`KalyeahTheme`): Clash (`assets/fonts/clash/`, the owner's font) is the one typeface (owner), with real lowercase letters (sentence case): headings, body copy, fields, buttons and HUD numbers. The roles differ by size, colour and tracking: display (titles, scores, numbers) untracked; body and buttons with a wider word gap (`WORD_GAP`); captions, section labels and muted detail lines (`CAPTION_FONT`, the `MutedLabel`/`SectionLabel` variations) tracked +2 px; paragraphs (descriptions, tips, hints) get `READING_LINE_SPACING` between their lines. Sizes come from the `UiMetrics` scale only (34 / 28 / 26 / 24 / 22). Warm cream headings; gold is the primary action; green confirms; red is destructive. Sentence case, thumb-sized targets (88 design px), no hover-only affordances.
- Icons (`scripts/ui/icons.gd`): **painted PNGs** in `assets/icons/` for the weapons, the title guava, the settings gear and the round button face (gold rim, navy face), all drawn with Pillow by `tools/art/make_assets.py` (4x supersampled, chunky dark outline, gradient, highlight, drop shadow). The old vector drawings remain as a fallback.
- HUD (`scripts/ui/hud/`, built in code by `MatchHud`): over each hero a health bar with one word over its top left corner: a plain (not slanted) status ("Stunned") while there is one, else "You" on your own bar (no names, no ring under the kids); heal numbers; the scoreboard pill at the top right (points and "FIRST TO 5"); the street minimap. Spec in `docs/GDD.md` "HUD". The skill buttons are art only with an ATK / CC / BLK tag.
- **Skill objects are real 3D models** (`scripts/view/art/weapon_model.gd`, `assets/models/weapons/*.glb`, made by `tools/blender/build_prop_model.py` from the owner's models, 1,800 to 2,500 triangles and a 512 px texture each): the **slipper** for both tsinelas (0.8 m light, 1.5 m heavy), the **can** for lata, the **marble** for bola, the **crumpled paper** for the papel trap (it lies on the road) and the **paper sheet** for the papel shield (stretched to the wall's 3 m x 1.6 m, standing on the ground). ## Asset pipeline (planned, P4 onward)
- Environment models (the kids are the owner's rigged models, see above) are authored with the Blender Python module (`bpy`, pip-installable in the cloud session) and exported as `.glb` into `assets/models/`. Generator scripts will live in `tools/blender/` so every asset can be regenerated.
- Each asset: chunky bevelled forms, hand-painted-style textures from a small palette atlas, baked ambient occlusion, vertex colours. Characters get a skeleton and baked animations.
- Budgets: APK under 150 MB (the `Android APK` workflow fails above it), 60 fps target on a recommended phone.
- Effects use CPU particles and simple mesh and ring effects.
- Replacing a procedural prop or character with its `.glb` must not change gameplay: same height and hitbox for the kids, same footprint for props.
- **A barangay street, like the reference video (owner):** one wide street whose play area is **the owner's tiled floor** (owner, round 11: "use this as floor for gameplay"): `assets/textures/cement.png`, soft beige-grey painted square tiles, covering all the ground the camera sees (owner): one whole picture exactly on the play area, repeated past both ends and on both sidewalk tops, lined up with it (`_add_cement_art()`, `_floor_patch()`), no other markings; with sidewalks (owner: no bricks), flat and level with the street (owner: no raised curb, `CURB_HEIGHT` 0), running right up to the street edge (no white curb strip or line, owner; the street slab and the sidewalk base are plain boxes, `LowPoly.plain_box`, because `box` lightens its rounded edges and that showed as a thin white line), house fronts and a sari-sari store with a striped umbrella on both sides (no bunting, owner). The street goes on past both ends (behind the barricade rows) to more houses. No floating white dots.
- **Bases and rescue areas:** each post stands in its corner inside a base (a faint fill and team-coloured tape, blue yours, red theirs, in the exact palette colours: unshaded and never softened or graded, see "Light and colour by asset class"; drawn under the kids). Visual pass: 34% fill and a **0.15 m chalk edge** (owner: thinner, it was 0.3 m; one constant, `StreetMap.TAPE_WIDTH`, for every base and rescue area, 1.4 to 3.7 px on screen at 390 and 720 wide). Each team has **one rescue area**, a rounded rectangle against the street's side edge with the same faint fill and tape as the base; there is no box inside it. **Tagged kids stand outside the street on the sidewalk strip beside it, facing in** (no new art: the kids and the cement are the existing ones; they stand in the camera's side margin, so the street's side props keep clear of them, the chicken cage stands at z 6).
- **Prop models must be whole (audit, owner: no cuts, seams or holes):** `build_prop_model.py` welds the vertices the glTF importer splits at UV and normal seams *before* decimating (decimating the split mesh tore the props into thousands of open edges: cracks and holes), checks the open edges against the source after, and bakes the colour with an extended margin so no black gaps bleed in as dark seams at small sizes. `tests/unit/test_prop_models.gd` fails if a prop is torn again. The source Meshy GLBs are the owner's and are not in the repo; rebuild with `python tools/blender/build_prop_model.py -- in.glb out.glb triangles texture_px`.
- **Kids are torn too, until rebuilt (audit):** the six kid GLBs have 1.3 to 1.7 open edges per triangle (about 1,200 loose pieces each, every texture island a tiny dot), the same fault the props had. `build_kid_game_model.py` now welds before decimating, wraps the same bake margin, and prints the open edges; on the one kid source on hand (the Meshy running boy) it gives 9 open edges where the game kid has 11,888. The six kids can only be rebuilt from the owner's full-resolution rigged models (not in the repo): `tests/unit/test_kid_models.gd` reports them as pending until then.
- **Play area read (owner):** the owner's floor picture is drawn untouched on the play area (27 x 48 m) and the same picture 22% darker (`StreetMap.OUTSIDE_SHADE` 0.78, patch `OutsideArt`) on all the ground outside it, so the street's edge reads at a glance. **The play area's four corners are rounded with the base's radius** (`MapLayout.base_corner`, 1 m): the floor is a fan mesh cut to the rounded outline (`StreetMap._play_floor`, the picture mapped over the whole area), the darker floor shows in the cut corners, and a base's rounded outer corner lies exactly on the street's. Zone marks and kids are not affected.

### Shared UI hierarchy

`UiMetrics` owns menu typography and spacing at the 720 px design width: title 34, body 28, action 26, detail 24 and caption 22 (the floor, `MIN_TEXT`). Use 32 px page margins, 24 px panel padding, 16 px component gaps and 24 px section gaps. Preserve negative space; reading content wraps or scrolls. Amber emphasizes primary actions and replacement slots; blue marks selected options/navigation, slate is secondary, green confirms, and red indicates destructive actions/errors. Active action text is contrast-tested. See [the HIG audit](audits/2026-10-19-ui-hig.md) for findings, validation and remaining accessibility work.


- **Display scale (one line, one family):** big lettered words use only `UiMetrics.HEADLINE` 40 (the intro, a name on a sheet, loading text), `DISPLAY` 56 (page and mode titles, the kid's name, coach titles, code letters), `HERO` 80 (the results banner, GO!/SCORED) and `SCORE` 120 (the results score); each step is about 1.4x so no two read as "almost the same" (main had 36, 40, 44, 52, 56, 72, 80, 96, 120). Every one wears the same letter style: a `UiSkin.LETTER_INK` (navy) outline of a sixth of the size (`letter_outline`) and a `LETTER_SHADOW` drop of a twelfth (`letter_shadow`), the median of the outlines main already used and the banner's 6 px at 72.
- **One corner family:** `UiSkin.RADIUS_S` 8 (dots, small tags, bar gloss), `RADIUS_M` 16 (fields, code boxes, rows, status strips, small tiles), `RADIUS_L` 24 (buttons, cards, tiles, flat surfaces: `KalyeahTheme.CORNER`), `RADIUS_XL` 32 (big panels, the toast, the tab bar, the profile chip, the card tray), or fully round with `UiSkin.round_radius(height)` (pills, meters, switches); on the same 8 px rhythm as the spacing. Buttons keep 24 (unchanged pixels); panels move 30 to 32 and odd 18/22/26/34 corners snap to the nearest step (at most 4 px, invisible at phone size, one family instead of sixteen numbers).
- **Button labels (one line, from the type scale only):** menu buttons `UiMetrics.ACTION` 26; dialog, secondary and primary in-match buttons `BODY` 28; Home's two hero buttons (Create / Join room) `TITLE` 34, the largest that fits "JOIN ROOM" at a third of the width; no bare font size on any button, label or drawn text (page title and scoreboard digits `HEADLINE` 40, first-to number and room slot numbers `TITLE` 34, overhead popup `BODY` 28).
- **Nested curves are parallel:** a shape inside another takes the outer radius minus the gap between them (the HUD's gloss inside its outline, an inner card frame); never a fixed floor.
- **Mid-body cards:** a card that sits in the middle of a big panel's body, not in its corner (a dialog's figures, the results score), takes `UiKit.NESTED_RADIUS` (`RADIUS_XL` - `NEST_GAP` = 16, `RADIUS_M`), never outer minus the panel's padding (that would go square); a card in the panel's corner sits `NEST_GAP` in, so it gets the same 16 and stays parallel.
- **Nine-slice:** every curve of a code-drawn nine-slice sits inside its corner slices and the stretched middle rows and columns are straight; a shape moved inside its texture (a pressed button's 9 px sink) grows the texture and that side's `texture_margin` by the same amount (a corner arc in the middle band stretches into a slanted side).
- **One focus ring:** `UiSkin.focus_ring(radius)` given the control's own radius; it stands `FOCUS_EXPAND` 4 px out with corners of radius + 4, so it follows the control's curve; one colour `FOCUS_COLOR` (cream, `KalyeahTheme.FOCUS` is the same) and a 4 px line (about 2 px on a 390 pt phone, the least a focus line can be and still be seen); `KalyeahTheme.focus_ring` only delegates.
