# Art, UI and audio

Look: **Clash of Clans design language** (soft, chunky, painted-looking, toy-like) on a **League of Legends camera**, in a Filipino street. Inspired by, never copied (`docs/PROJECT.md` 3.8). The target is the owner's reference image; the work to get there is the visual overhaul in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## Where we are
The current art is a first pass built **in code**: procedural meshes, vector icons and synthesised sound, with no asset files. It is soft-shaded and readable but not final. The plan replaces it piece by piece with real assets made in Blender (see "Asset pipeline").

### 3D (procedural, current)
- `scripts/view/art/low_poly.gd` (`LowPoly`): bevelled boxes, smooth cylinders and spheres, gable roofs, with vertex colours and **one shared matte material** (wrap lighting, soft rim, no hard speculars). Cartoon outlines come from an inverted shell (`add_outline`).
- `scripts/view/art/palette.gd` (`Palette`): the shared colour palette.
- `scripts/view/art/props.gd` (`Props`): sari-sari store, houses (one and two storey), laundry lines, tarpaulins, basketball ring, jeepney, market umbrella, the cover pieces (crates, bench, sawhorse, sign board), plants and trees, manholes, hopscotch, the thrown weapon objects, and the polymorph can. Meshes are cached and kept under a vertex budget by a test.
- **Cardboard walls** are the bahay-bahayan: disassembled boxes as single flat sheets (creases, tape, printing, curled flaps, a torn corner) leaning on sticks. Never stacked boxes.
- `scripts/view/street_map.gd` (`StreetMap`): asphalt, lane paint, a cross street with zebra crossings, curbs, sidewalks, house rows, base pads and posts, props. The lane geometry comes from `MapLayout` and never changes for looks.
- Lighting: one sun with soft shadows, 2x MSAA, saturation grading, a light distance haze.

### Characters (current)
- `scripts/view/art/kid_model.gd` (`KidModel`): all six kids are **real 3D models** in `assets/models/characters/<kid>.glb` (rigged GLB, ~9k triangles, one 1024 px baked texture), made light for phones by `tools/blender/build_kid_game_model.py` from the owner's full-resolution rigged kids (not stored in the repo). The skeleton uses `.L` / `.R` names (`hips`, `pelvis`, `spine.01…`, `chest`, `neck`, `head`, `shoulder`, `upper_arm` + twist, `forearm` + twist, `hand`, fingers, `thigh`, `shin`, `foot`, `toe`, spring bones for hair, skirt, pigtails and the belt bag) under a ground `root`; elbow and knee corrective blend shapes keep the joints from collapsing (the full models also have hip and shoulder ones, left out on phones because each blend shape is a full mesh copy). Clips (30 fps): `idle_alert`, `run`, `throw`, `throw_run`, `cast_one_hand`, `cast_run`, `scratch_head`, `trip`, `stunned`, `airborne_fall`, `airborne_fall_land`, `knockdown`; the game plays them under its own names (`idle`, `run`, `cast`, `spell`, `stun`, `airborne`, `land`, `ko`, `KidModel.CLIP_FOR`). In game the run cycle is paced by the ground speed (one cycle per 1.6 m) so the feet do not skate; a cast plays at the weapon's cast time, as a throw, or a one-hand spell for heals, shields and traps (`KidModel.is_spell`); a cast on the move plays `throw_run` / `cast_run` on the upper body only (a second, non-deterministic `AnimationPlayer`) so the legs keep running; airborne kids flail and land with a squash; a knockout plays the kid's own knock-down (they land on their back); the clips play in place (the sim owns positions). The stun stars are little 3D stars. **Junjun's own animation (owner):** his model (`CharacterDef.native_animation = true`) plays its own clips at their own speed: the run is not paced to the ground speed (and the footsteps follow his run cycle), a cast shows his throw or cast clip at normal speed for its whole length (not squeezed into the weapon's cast time), and a spell starts at the start of its clip. The other kids keep the game's pacing. `ModelLook` gives every model the matte look the owner approved: roughness 1, no metal, no normal map, wrapped diffuse, a soft rim, albedo x0.65. **Junjun** is the owner's rebuilt-rig boy (`Boy_rebuilt_rig.glb`, Mixamo skeleton, 10 clips): `build_kid_game_model.py` renames its bones and clips to the game's names, welds and decimates it to 14,000 triangles (the shorts and tank top overlap at the waist, and fewer triangles leave white blotches there) and bakes one 1024 px texture with a tight bake range (`KID_CAGE=0.002 KID_RAY=0.006`). He has no landing clip, so a landing plays nothing extra. Kids without their own model use Junjun's. No team colour on the kid: the health bar tells teams apart. HUD cards use `assets/portraits/<kid>.png` (rendered and cropped by `tools/blender/render_portrait.py`).
- All six share one height and proportion; the hitbox is always `GameRules.player_radius`. Outfits, hair and skin tones (varied across the roster) come from `CharacterDef` (`data/characters/*.tres`). A team bandana, armband and ground ring show the team.
- Written with respect (PROJECT 3.2): identity through clothes and style only.

## HUD and UI
- **Skin and kit (UI rebuild, phase 1).** `UiSkin` (`scripts/ui/ui_skin.gd`) draws every button and panel in code: small anti-aliased textures rendered once from a rounded-rectangle distance field (gradient face, gloss, lit top edge, dark lip, outline, drop shadow in the padding), used as 9-slice style boxes. Button variants gold (the one main action), blue, green, red and slate; panel kinds glass, raised, inset and night. `KalyeahTheme.style_button` and `painted_panel` delegate to it, so every screen wears it. Kit: `ui_kit.gd` (page header, card, chip, setting row), `ui_tab_bar.gd`, `ui_segmented.gd`, `ui_toggle.gd`, `ui_profile_chip.gd`, `ui_backdrop.gd`. The owner's button art can replace the textures without touching a screen. Direction and critique: `docs/audits/2026-10-20-ui-rebuild-critique.md`.
- **Feedback and profile (round 7).** `UiToast` (`ui_toast.gd`) answers an action with a short pill above the tab bar (ok, info, warn, error; one at a time, its own canvas layer, never takes a touch, no rise or fade with Reduce motion). `UiStatusStrip` (`ui_status_strip.gd`) dresses a screen's status line: amber while waiting, red for a problem, blue for a note. `ProfileSheet` (`profile_sheet.gd`) is the name and face picker. `UiGallery` (`ui_gallery.gd`, Settings, UI gallery) shows every component in every state with the measured contrast of each chip; a new component is not finished until it is there. Chip fills (`UiKit.CHIP_*`) all keep white text at 4.5:1 or better.
- **Code boxes and the walkthrough (round 8).** `UiCodeBoxes` (`ui_code_boxes.gd`) is the room code as eight boxes: an invisible LineEdit over them takes the keyboard and paste, the boxes show it, the next box is lit gold, a wrong code turns them red; read-only it shows the code in the room header. `CoachMarks` (`hud/coach_marks.gd`, shader `shaders/coach_spot.gdshader`) is the first-run walkthrough: a dim with round gold-ringed holes over the joystick and the weapon buttons and a card with Next and Skip. `HowToPlayPage` shows the same steps in Settings.
- **Skin speed (round 9).** Textures are drawn left half then mirrored (pixel-identical, about 2 to 3 times faster), and the ones the first screens use are built one a frame behind the boot screen's loading bar (`UiSkin.warm_next`, 20% of the bar), so no first tap pays for them. A test fails if a first screen draws a texture that is not on the warm list.
- Typography (`KalyeahTheme`): Bungee (`assets/fonts/bungee/`, OFL) is the one typeface (owner): headings, body copy, fields, buttons and HUD numbers. Its capitals are heavy and tight, so the roles differ by size, colour and tracking: display (titles, scores, numbers) untracked; body and buttons with a wider word gap (`WORD_GAP`); captions, section labels and muted detail lines (`CAPTION_FONT`, the `MutedLabel`/`SectionLabel` variations) tracked +2 px; paragraphs (descriptions, tips, hints) get `READING_LINE_SPACING` between their lines. Sizes come from the `UiMetrics` scale only (34 / 28 / 26 / 24 / 22). Warm cream headings; gold is the primary action; green confirms; red is destructive. Sentence case, thumb-sized targets (88 design px), no hover-only affordances.
- Icons (`scripts/ui/icons.gd`): **painted PNGs** in `assets/icons/` for the weapons, the title guava, the settings gear and the round button face (gold rim, navy face), all drawn with Pillow by `tools/art/make_assets.py` (4x supersampled, chunky dark outline, gradient, highlight, drop shadow). The old vector drawings remain as a fallback.
- HUD (`scripts/ui/hud/`, built in code by `MatchHud`): over each hero a health bar, an *italic* status above it ("Stunned") (no names); heal numbers; the scoreboard pill at the top right (points and "FIRST TO 5"); the street minimap. Spec in `docs/GDD.md` "HUD". The skill buttons are art only with an ATK / CC / BLK tag.
- **Skill objects are real 3D models** (`scripts/view/art/weapon_model.gd`, `assets/models/weapons/*.glb`, made by `tools/blender/build_prop_model.py` from the owner's models, 1,800 to 2,500 triangles and a 512 px texture each): the **slipper** for both tsinelas (0.8 m light, 1.5 m heavy), the **can** for lata, the **marble** for bola, the **crumpled paper** for the papel trap (it lies on the road) and the **paper sheet** for the papel shield (stretched to the wall's 3 m x 1.6 m, standing on the ground). More owner models: the **spinning top** for trumpo (spins 6 turns a second), the **jacks** (`spikes.glb`) scattered as five clusters over the jacks field's tinted circle, the **meteor** that falls from the sky onto the rock-meteor circle and lands as its delay ends, the **scissors** that snip forward through a gunting cone (the tinted wedge stays), the **soil block** that rises, stands and collapses as the langit lupa wall, and the **green cross** that floats up over a healed kid. The **light rock** (bato light) is the meteor's stone, thrown small (0.55 m), until the owner makes a separate rock. Every thrown or placed skill object is now a 3D model; only the flat tinted circles and cone wedges under them are drawn in code.
- **The main screen** (`scripts/ui/title_screen.gd`): the owner's diamond picture behind (`UiBackdrop`, with a glow in the chosen mode's colour); the logo and a profile chip (tap to change the name) on top; the mode carousel as the hero (`scripts/ui/menu_carousel.gd`: large 2.5:4 cards, the chosen one framed in light and floating a little, the others stacked behind it); the mode's name, one short phrase, page dots and the explicit Create room / Join room (or Start practice) actions; and a bottom tab bar (Play, Book, History, Settings) that swaps the middle of the screen while the chrome stays put. Android Back closes a tab to Play, and on Play asks to quit. Icons are drawn in code (`scripts/ui/nav_icon.gd`) as stand-ins: the owner's own pictures at `assets/ui/sections/<id>.png` replace them. Each mode card is the owner's picture (`assets/ui/cards/<id>.webp`); a card without a picture falls back to a painted card. Pages: `codex_page.gd` (Characters | Weapons, both display and description only, never selection; **round 10:** a By game section shows the sentence about that street game (`data/rules/street_games.tres`), a weapon sheet shows damage or healing, cooldown and range as bars (a full bar is the biggest of any weapon) with the exact number beside and *The game* sentence under, a kid sheet shows the outfit palette as swatches (clothes and style, never skin tone), and History has Played, Won and Streak above a mode filter; Characters is two columns of large faces and a tap opens a sheet with the kid's description (`CharacterDef.description`); the Weapons tab groups the weapons two ways, By game (the street game each is inspired by) and By type (Attack, Control, Block, Heal), as headed sections of two-column tiles, and a tap opens a detail sheet with the picture, text and damage, cooldown and range: `weapons_page.gd`), `history_page.gd`, `settings_page.gd`. Notches and the home bar stay clear.
- **Lobby** (`scripts/ui/lobby_screen.gd`, presentation helpers in `lobby_widgets.gd`): reuses the diamond background behind a dark scrim, a compact back/title header, grouped scroll-safe surfaces, labelled high-contrast fields, segmented team-size and BO3 / BO5 / BO7 choices, a large eight-character room code, bordered team cards and concise player-state tags. - **Round 11 (in-match chrome).** The scoreboard frame, the opening objective card, the tagged sheet and the HUD status pills (`HudPill`, `UiSkin.pill`) are `UiSkin` night and raised panels, with no control moved. The loadout's weapon cards carry three compact stat bars (damage or healing, cooldown, range, scaled against every weapon; the amber line under them keeps the exact numbers) and a *Suggested* button next to the hint offers a homing attack plus a block (`WeaponPick.suggestion`) while fewer than two weapons are picked. The pick countdown ring is 104 px and pulses in its last seconds (steady with Reduce motion).
- **Match presentation:** the Bungee score card groups team-relative points, the clock and first-to context in one gold-rimmed top-right element. Objective, capture and result announcements use the same display typography as the menus. The pre-match kid and game rows scroll horizontally when a narrow phone cannot fit every option. - Banners pop in, the camera shakes on hits and wall breaks, buttons click.
- **Hits you can feel (round 13).** A red glow runs round the screen edge when you are hit (stronger and longer when tagged), a weapon button swells and washes bright when it is ready, and a blocked button names its cause (STUNNED, CAN). All three are constants in `HurtPulse` and `TouchButton`; Reduce motion halves the glow and stops the swell.
- **Depth (round 18).** Weapons are named "Name" or "Name · Role" ("Bato · Light", "Papel · Trap", "Lupa · Soil Block"), no brackets, and
  the short names are all different. The UI gallery is a developer page: seven quick taps on the Settings title switch it on (and off),
  and the web build opens with it on for `?dev=1`. Every control with no words of its own has an `accessibility_name` (a screen reader
  reads it); `UiA11y` and the lint test keep that true. All the words are in `data/i18n/strings.csv` (`tools/i18n/README.md`); a Language

- **The match at a glance (round 17).** The minimap is 130 x 260 px by default (the old 100 x 200 was about 54 pt on a phone; small 100 x 200 and large 160 x 320 are a Settings choice), its faces are 9 px at the base size and everything in it scales together. - **Floors (round 15, `UiMetrics.MIN_TEXT` and `MIN_TARGET`).** No text under **22 px** and no tappable control under **80 px** tall (88 px is the standard), on any screen; the weapon buttons' type tag is 20 px and the status over a kid 22. `tests/unit/test_ui_lint.gd` opens Home in every mode, every page and sheet, the lobby forms, the room views, the loadout, the match, the match menu and the tagged sheet, and fails on a violation or a banned word ("skill", "dash", "dummies", "Lobby", three dots, a bullet). Words: one ellipsis (…), one separator (·), "Best of N" for the format, "weapons" (never "skills"), a name is shown as typed (the display font is all capitals, so names use the body font), and the first key press, not a touch, brings a dialog's focus ring.
- **Awareness (round 14).** Below 30% health the screen edge breathes red; a thin bar fills along the score card's lower edge while a capture runs (light blue yours, light red theirs); a gold marker bobs over the rescue box when it is on screen; the minimap rings a teammate's box and a captured base. ## Asset pipeline (planned, P4 onward)
- Environment models (the kids are the owner's rigged models, see above) are authored with the Blender Python module (`bpy`, pip-installable in the cloud session) and exported as `.glb` into `assets/models/`. Generator scripts will live in `tools/blender/` so every asset can be regenerated.
- Each asset: chunky bevelled forms, hand-painted-style textures from a small palette atlas, baked ambient occlusion, vertex colours. Characters get a skeleton and baked animations.
- Budgets: web build under 50 MB, APK under 150 MB, 60 fps target on a recommended phone.
- Effects use CPU particles (web-safe) and simple mesh and ring effects.
- Replacing a procedural prop or character with its `.glb` must not change gameplay: same height and hitbox for the kids, same footprint for props.
- **A barangay street, like the reference video (owner):** one wide street whose play area is **plain, flat, dark grey cement** (owner: no tiles or markings; `CEMENT` in `street_map.gd`). **Realistic cement detail is an owner-made 2D image:** put it at `assets/textures/cement.png` (1:1, e.g. 2048 x 2048, top-down, dark grey cement with hairline cracks, patched repairs, stains, worn edges, no perspective or shadows) and the map lays it once over the whole play area (stretched, never tiled); with plain gray cement sidewalks (owner: no bricks), raised 0.4 m above the street (`CURB_HEIGHT`), running right up to the street edge (no white curb strip or line, owner; the street slab and the sidewalk base are plain boxes, `LowPoly.plain_box`, because `box` lightens its rounded edges and that showed as a thin white line), house fronts and a sari-sari store with a striped umbrella on both sides (no bunting, owner). The street goes on past both ends (behind the barricade rows) to more houses. No floating white dots.
- **Bases and boxes:** each post stands in its corner inside a base circle (a faint fill and team-coloured tape, blue yours, red theirs, softened toward grey; drawn under the kids). Round each tagged box is its **rescue area** (the same faint fill and tape as the base circle, on the two sides that face the street). Each team's tagged box is a square flush in a corner with **no tint and no tape**: two cardboard-wall models close its sides that face the street (the same `cardboard_wall` model as the cover, scenery only).
- **Prop models must be whole (audit, owner: no cuts, seams or holes):** `build_prop_model.py` welds the vertices the glTF importer splits at UV and normal seams *before* decimating (decimating the split mesh tore the props into thousands of open edges: cracks and holes), checks the open edges against the source after, and bakes the colour with an extended margin so no black gaps bleed in as dark seams at small sizes. `tests/unit/test_prop_models.gd` fails if a prop is torn again. The source Meshy GLBs are the owner's and are not in the repo; rebuild with `python tools/blender/build_prop_model.py -- in.glb out.glb triangles texture_px`.
- **Kids are torn too, until rebuilt (audit):** the six kid GLBs have 1.3 to 1.7 open edges per triangle (about 1,200 loose pieces each, every texture island a tiny dot), the same fault the props had. `build_kid_game_model.py` now welds before decimating, wraps the same bake margin, and prints the open edges; on the one kid source on hand (the Meshy running boy) it gives 9 open edges where the game kid has 11,888. The six kids can only be rebuilt from the owner's full-resolution rigged models (not in the repo): `tests/unit/test_kid_models.gd` reports them as pending until then.
- **Cover and street props are 3D models** (`assets/models/props/*.glb`, `PropModel`, made by `tools/blender/build_prop_model.py` from the owner's Meshy models: decimated to 0.7-4k triangles, one baked base-colour texture, matte `ModelLook`): folded cardboard walls are the only cover inside the play area; the barrier rows across both ends line up a fishball cart, a bench, stacked crates, the SLOW DOWN SCHOOL ZONE sign, a basketball hoop, stacked truck tyres, an ironing board and the wooden A-frame, each at its real height and facing the play area (`BARRIER_HEIGHTS` in `street_map.gd`); the electric post at each base is the same kind of model, turned to face the middle of the play area (no outline: the base circle's team tint tells the bases apart, owner); owner: no power poles along the left and right sides. The store umbrella, houses, trees and the rest stay low-poly built in code (`Props`).

## Room-flow UI polish

Bungee is the only typeface. The hotspot header has no room code. Replace the large empty map panel with a compact Agawan Base header; give team names, You/Host/Ready states and character choice more space. Essential room targets are 88+ design pixels high. Character cards scroll horizontally and reuse the owner's face art across the lobby and character book, avoiding cropped model portraits. Content can scroll independently of Start/Ready. The preview label is persistent; Settings isolates Quit as its destructive action. No new artwork is generated.


### Loadout readability and control feedback

Loadout setup uses an opaque navy surface, amber selected slots, the existing painted weapon art, and scrollable 3:4 character cards. Descriptions and weapon names use 34 px body text at the 720 px design width; statistics use 28 px. Weapon cards grow with wrapped content. Slots remain above the scrolling list, with an 88 px confirmation action below it. The match HUD shows one contextual hint above the controls for auto-run, aim/cancel or casting restrictions. Connection recovery uses a persistent central notice until server confirmation.


### Shared UI hierarchy

`UiMetrics` owns menu typography and spacing at the 720 px design width: body 34, action 32, detail 28, caption 24 and heading 40. Use 32 px page margins, 24 px panel padding, 16 px component gaps and 24 px section gaps. Preserve negative space; reading content wraps or scrolls. Amber emphasizes primary actions and replacement slots; blue marks selected options/navigation, slate is secondary, green confirms, and red indicates destructive actions/errors. Active action text is contrast-tested. See [the HIG audit](audits/2026-10-19-ui-hig.md) for findings, validation and remaining accessibility work.


### Readability and reduced motion

Use one readable type scale; the owner removed in-game text-size modes. Wrap or scroll rather than trim instructions. Actions use the body face and restrained elevation; keep branded display type for headings and HUD counters. Group related content and leave negative space between task areas. Menu focus uses the shared visible ring; modal navigation stays within enabled controls and scrolls the focused item into view. Reduced motion removes decorative UI pops, carousel snapping animation, shake, animated grain and NPC sway; the opening show uses the normal gameplay camera. Preserve telegraphs, character action animation and authoritative timing. See [accessibility follow-up](audits/2026-10-19-accessibility.md).
