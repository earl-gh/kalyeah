# Kalyeah lore (draft for the owner to edit and approve)

Rules from `docs/SPEC.md` §7: kids aged 8 to 12 from rival puroks settle disputes through street-game showdowns every
summer; skills are their imagination made real. Every kid is written with respect: no jokes about looks, family, money
or where they live. The owner's models define each kid's look; nothing here restyles them.

## Puroks (owner pick: 4 puroks, 3 kids each, mixed roles)
Each purok is one map's home turf, so every map is someone's neighbourhood.

| Purok | Home map | Kids (role) |
|---|---|---|
| **Uptown** (barangay hall, plaza, court) | Barangay | Narda (Striker), Jopay (Defender), Kikay (Seeker) |
| **Palengke** (the market) | Palengke | Inggo (Striker), Karding (Seeker), Pamela (Controller) |
| **Pantalan** (the pier) | Pier | Jumong (Striker), Oriang (Defender), Domeng (Controller) |
| **Tubuhan** (cane fields and paddies) | Hacienda | Paeng (Defender), Manoy (Seeker), Tere (Controller) |

Kids from the same purok are friends; each kid also has one **rival** of the same role from another purok:
Narda ↔ Jumong, Inggo ↔ Manoy, Jopay ↔ Paeng, Oriang ↔ Pamela, Kikay ↔ Karding, Tere ↔ Domeng.

## Voice (owner pick: playful trash talk, never mean)
Short Taglish barks like real kids at play ("Taya ka!", "Bagal mo naman!"); never insults about looks, family or money.
When friends or rivals are in the same match they talk to each other at the start and on tags and rescues.

## Story in the game (owner picks)
- **Codex** chapters per kid, unlocked with mastery, narrated by the Kuya/Ate neighbour.
- **Friend and rival lines** in matches (above).

## Kids
Each kid has their own idle, run style and victory pose, all at the same animation quality (owner pick). Dream jobs feed
the "dream jobs" skin line.

| Kid | Personality | Home and family | Dream job | Idle | Victory pose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Narda** | Quick and cheeky; the fastest runner in Uptown | Lives above the family's bakery by the plaza | Track athlete | Bounces on toes, spins a slipper on one finger | Flips a slipper and catches it on a foot |
