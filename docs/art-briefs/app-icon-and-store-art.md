- **Animation beats (30 Hz) or message:** Static.
- **Stand-in it replaces:** `assets/app_icon/icon.png` (owner's Junjun face) where the owner wants an update.
- **How it is accepted:** No Supercell or Riot names, layouts or assets; Android adaptive icon foreground and background layers both supplied.
- **Priority:** P2 (before the store release, M6).
