# The 12 kids: game-ready meshes for rigging and animation (modelling spec)

> **Owner decision (2026-11-19):** the current 12 Meshy kids are kept and cleaned up **by script** (reduction to the
> budgets, separated parts where possible, texture layout, rig on the shared skeleton) instead of a manual rebuild.
> This spec stays the target the script is checked against; where a kid fails a rule (joint rings, separate fingers),
> the gap is reported with screenshots so the owner can decide.

- **Kind and why:** Blender (3D model). The kids are seen from every angle, run, jump, throw and are shown large on the main screen. This is advice for the owner's artist or tool; nothing here is generated by agents.
- **What they are today:** `assets/models/raw/<Kid>.glb` (Narda, Jumong, Inggo, Jopay, Oriang, Paeng, Kikay, Karding, Manoy, Tere, Pamela, Domeng) are sculpt-density meshes (55k to 320k triangles) with body, clothes and hair fused into one skin, fused or missing fingers, auto-generated skeletons and a different rest pose each. They keep their look but cannot bend, swing or run at phone frame rates. **Keep the sculpts: they are the source for the look and for baking detail.**
- **What to deliver, per kid:** one `.glb` (or `.blend`) with clean, separate, low-triangle meshes in the standard pose below. **No skeleton, no weights, no animation**: rigging, weights and clips are done after delivery with the scripts in `tools/blender/kids/` on the roster's shared skeleton.
- **Priority:** P0 (blocks the 12-kid roster, the shared clip set and cloth/hair motion).

## 1. Parts (separate objects, named exactly)

| Object | Contents | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Body` | head, neck, torso, arms, hands, legs, feet as one closed skin | Skin continues ~2 cm under every clothing edge; skin fully hidden under clothes is deleted (no hidden torso under a shirt) |
| `Hair` | scalp cap + hair shapes | Ponytails, braids and long locks as their own connected strips so they can swing |
| `Top`, `Bottom` | shirt/sando/sweater, shorts/skirt | Loose enough to clear the skin by 3 to 6 mm; open at sleeves, neck, hem and legs (a real gap at the armpit) |
| `Footwear` | tsinelas | Separate from the foot |
| `Acc_<Name>` | glasses, headband, bow, bracelet, necklace, cape, cap | One object each |
| `Eyes`, `Teeth` (optional) | eyeballs, teeth/tongue | Only if the face should blink and talk later |

## 2. Standard pose (identical for all 12)

- Standing upright, feet flat, soles on the ground at the origin, facing the front (Blender −Y), symmetric left/right.
- **A-pose:** upper arms 40° below horizontal, straight out to the sides (no forward or backward swing).
- **Elbows** bent 5° with the crease facing straight forward; **knees** bent 3° pointing straight forward.
- **Palms** face the thighs, thumbs forward; **five separate fingers per hand**, relaxed, slightly spread, with clear gaps between them all the way to the knuckle webbing.
- **Feet** point straight forward, hip width apart.
- Real proportions in metres; each kid keeps its own height and build (state each kid's intended height so `unity/Data/characters__<kid>.json` can hold it).

## 3. Topology

- **Quads, even density**, edge loops following the body: around eyes and mouth, around shoulders, and **rings around every joint**:
  - elbows and knees: 3 rings each (the middle one on the crease, extra spacing on the outer side);
  - wrists, ankles, neck, waist: 2 to 3 rings;
  - shoulders and hips: 3 rings fanning into the torso;
  - each finger: 2 rings per knuckle; palm loops running across the knuckle line.
- No triangles or poles on a joint crease; no fused or intersecting surfaces; every part a closed or cleanly open-edged mesh; no loose vertices; normals facing out.
- Clothes follow the same joint rings as the skin under them (sleeve rings at the elbow, shorts rings at the hip and knee) so both bend together.
- Skirts and capes: evenly spaced vertical columns of quads (12 to 16 around a skirt) so spring bones can swing them.

## 4. Budgets (per kid, whole character)

| Version | Triangles | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Match | **15,000 to 25,000** | 10 kids on screen on a mid-range Android phone |
| Showcase (required, `docs/GRAPHICS.md`) | up to 60,000 | Home, kid select and kid page, one kid at a time |

- Rough split for the match version: body 8 to 12k (each hand ~1.2k, head ~3k), hair 2 to 4k, clothes 3 to 6k, the rest ≤ 2k.
- **Materials:** at most 2 (opaque + one for hair/alpha if needed).
- **Textures:** one 2048 px base colour atlas for the whole kid; optional 1024 px normal map and 1024 px roughness/metal/occlusion map. Bake the sculpt's detail (folds, knuckles, hair strands) from the raw model into these maps. No baked-in shadows or lighting in the base colour.
- **UVs:** one clean layout, no overlaps (mirrored halves may share space), seams hidden (inner arms and legs, under hair, along clothing seams), 4 px padding at 2048.
- File size target: under 6 MB per kid.

## 5. Face (required: kids blink, react and talk in matches, `docs/GRAPHICS.md`)

Seven shapes sculpted on the final `Body` head, face vertices only: `blink_L`, `blink_R`, `smile`, `open`, `angry`, `surprised`, `hurt`. Rings around the eyes and mouth (section 3) are what make these possible, so include those rings even if the shapes come later.

## 6. Look

Unchanged from the raw sculpts and `docs/ART.md`: same faces, clothes, colours and accessories. Silhouette at 64 px tall must still identify each kid.

## 7. How it is accepted

Checked by script and by eye before any rigging starts:

1. Object names and part list match section 1; no skeleton or animation in the file.
2. Pose measurements: arm 40° ± 2° below horizontal, elbow and knee creases facing forward ± 5°, palms to thighs ± 10°, feet forward ± 5°, soles at 0, left/right symmetric.
3. Five separate fingers per hand; no faces shared between fingers past the webbing.
4. Triangle, material, texture and file-size budgets of section 4.
5. No open edges except the intended clothing openings; no faces passing through other parts in the rest pose; clothes clear the skin by ≥ 3 mm.
6. Joint rings present at every joint in section 3.
7. Turnaround renders next to the raw sculpt at 1280×720 and 844×390: the kid still looks like itself.

After acceptance: the kid is rigged on the shared skeleton, weighted, checked against the game's real clips (`tools/blender/kids/clip_preview.py`), given hair/cloth springs and corrective shapes, and only then brought into the game.

## 8. Order

Start with **one pilot kid** (suggested: **Narda**: skirt, long hair, glasses and sleeves cover the hardest cases) and run it through rigging and the real clips before doing the other eleven, so any change to this spec is made once.
