# Softer body font (recommendation, not an asset to make)

> Owner decision: Bungee is the one typeface today. This is advice only.

- **Kind and why:** Licensed font file, chosen by the owner.
- **Where the player sees it and for how long:** Paragraphs, descriptions and tips (not titles or numbers, which stay Bungee).
- **Size and file format:** One TTF/OTF in `assets/fonts/<name>/` with its licence file.
- **Silhouette test:** Lower case readable at 22 px (`UiMetrics.CAPTION`) on 390 px wide phones.
- **Palette (tokens):** Text colours from `KalyeahTheme` unchanged.
- **Animation beats (30 Hz) or message:** None.
- **Stand-in it replaces:** Bungee as the body face (owner rule today: Bungee only).
- **How it is accepted:** Candidates under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (commercial use and embedding allowed): **Baloo 2**, **Nunito**, **Fredoka**. Check the licence file ships with the font; render the How to play page at 390 px.
- **Priority:** P3.
