# Jacks (jackstone spikes), plastic jacks in four colours

- **Kind and why:** Blender (3D model). A jack lies on the street in a field, seen from the 45 degree camera, so it needs real depth and round balls that turn in the light. This is advice for the owner's artist; nothing here is generated.
- **What it is today:** `assets/models/weapons/spikes.glb` is the owner's earlier Meshy model, edited by instruction to match the owner's photo of plastic jacks (2026-10-31): its vertices moved onto a six-pronged jack by `tools/art/reshape_spikes.py` (same 3,011 vertices, 3,000 triangles, UVs, material and node; 48 stray triangles that crossed between prongs are collapsed to a point), its picture rewritten to a plain light plastic. The old model had eight prongs; a photo jack has six, so one prong on each side folds into the hub. It has no moulding seam, no glossy highlight and a rough hub: a model made on purpose would.
- **Reference (binding):** the photo of four small plastic jacks on a dark surface: yellow, pink, green, orange. Each is six slim round shafts that thicken a little toward the middle, with a round ball at every tip; a small round hub; no spikes, no cones, no metal. It lies on three of its balls (the prongs are 35.26 degrees off the street).
  - Proportions (hub centre to the outside of a ball is 1): ball radius 0.25, shaft radius 0.10 at the ball and 0.19 at the hub, ball centres 0.75 from the hub.
- **Colours:** the game paints each cluster of a field one colour of the photo, cleaned up the Clash way (saturated, bright, no grey): yellow `#F4E43A`, pink `#FF4F9A`, green `#45E06C`, orange `#FF6A1F` (`WeaponModel.SPIKE_COLOURS`). The model's picture is plain light plastic (`#F4F4F6`) so one model serves all four; the painted light (lighter on top, deep cool shade underneath) and a contour in a deep tone of the cluster's own colour are the game's (the cluster's colour rides in its vertex colours, so the model keeps one look). A field has four clusters, one in each colour.
- **Technical:** one mesh, one material, a plain base colour, at most 3,000 triangles, resting on the street at y = 0 with its middle on the origin in x and z, about one unit across (the game draws it 0.63 m wide). Keep the normals pointing out of the balls and shafts (the contour is pushed along them).
- **Drop it in:** `python3 tools/blender/build_prop_model.py in.glb out.glb 3000 512 keep_uv`, replace `assets/models/weapons/spikes.glb` and `spikes_prop_base_color.jpg` (same names), and run `tests/unit/test_spikes_shape.gd`.
- **How it is accepted:** at 390 px wide, from the game camera, four clusters on the street read as four coloured plastic jacks like the photo; no streaks near the hub.
- **Priority:** P3.
