# Trumpo (spinning top), the bare wooden one on a steel nail

- **Kind and why:** Blender (3D model). It is thrown, spins about its vertical axis and is seen from the 45 degree camera, so it needs real depth and a round body that turns in the light. This is advice for the owner's artist; nothing here is generated.
- **What it is today:** `assets/models/weapons/trumpo.glb` is the owner's earlier Meshy model, edited by instruction to match the owner's photo: its vertices moved onto the photo's outline (same 1,640 vertices, 2,199 triangles, UVs, material and node), its picture's pixels rewritten to plain wood and steel. It has no real grain, no steel head on the nail and no lathe lines: a model made on purpose would.
- **Reference (binding, the owner's words: "its shape must be exactly like this"):** the photo of a bare, unpainted, light wooden spinning top with a thin steel nail for a tip. No rope, no paint, no bottle cap. Measured on the photo (478 x 720 px), as fractions of the whole height (683 px from the top of the wood to the nail's point):
  - Body: from the top (px 22) to the wooden tip (px 455) is 0.634 of the height. Widest point at px 190, **39% of the body's height below its top**, 444 px wide, so **0.65 of the whole height** and **1.02 of the body's height**.
  - Top: a small flat (about 60% of the widest width where the shoulder has curved 7% down) with a shallow lip or recess at the very top; the shoulder above the widest point is rounded and convex.
  - Below the widest point the sides taper in a gently convex, almost straight cone to a **short blunt wooden tip, 9% of the widest width** (about 40 px).
  - Pin: a thin round steel nail, **7% of the widest width** (32 to 34 px), **0.366 of the whole height** long (250 px), ending in a small chamfered point.
- **Colours:** wood plain pale beech `#E0B27A`, light `#F2CF9E`, dark `#C28A4E`, with a gentle figure and thin darker growth lines (the photo's median is `#A07A50` in dim light); pin steel grey `#6A6F73`, darker at the point. No paint, no bands, no stripes, no red or navy, no cream. Flat and painted, not photographic (see `weapon-textures.md`: few flat colours, painted light, no gloss). Note the weapon look turns shade violet by default; the trumpo keeps a warm brown shade (`WeaponLook.WOOD_SHADOW`) so the wood stays wood.
- **Technical:** one mesh, one material, a 512 or 1024 px base colour texture with a clean UV layout, at most 3,000 triangles, axis vertical, the nail's point on the ground at the origin, one object (the game scales it to a 1.12 m longest side and spins it about Y). Its contour is drawn by pushing the mesh out along its normals, so keep the nail's normals pointing up and out (a thin pin with radial normals gets a contour three times its width).
- **Drop it in:** `python3 tools/blender/build_prop_model.py in.glb out.glb <triangles> 1024 keep_uv`, then replace `assets/models/weapons/trumpo.glb` (same name, same rules as today). Check the picture first with `python3 tools/art/check_weapon_texture.py picture.png`, and run `tests/unit/test_trumpo_shape.gd` and `test_trumpo_palette.gd`: they hold the proportions above (with tolerances) and the wood and steel palette, so a model that departs from the photo fails them, and so does one with paint on it.
- **How it is accepted:** at 390 px wide, from the game camera, it reads as a plain wooden top on a nail, never as a rope top or a painted one; side by side with the photo at the same height the outline matches within a few percent of the widest width; one turn of the spin (6 turns a second) shows nothing flicker.
- **Priority:** P2.
