# Audit: what to do after the UI rounds

Written after round 14 (#195 and #196 open). Findings come from the code, the workflows, the GDD and the earlier
audits, not from guesses. Ranked by how much each one blocks the goal: **a good Android game that can be entered in
the competition**.

## Where the project stands

- The interface is built through round 14 (kit, Home, Book, results, in-match feedback and awareness). 620 tests.
- Gameplay is complete as designed: 14 weapon files (light and heavy variants) over 9 shapes, tag/rescue/capture, best of 3/5/7, six kids.
- Every UI round says "not checked on Android hardware", and the [Android validation record](2026-10-19-android-validation.md)
  lists physical touch, lifecycle, network and populated 3v3 performance as unverified. **No evidence in the repo
  that the game has been played on a real Android phone**, and the two newest rounds have not been felt by anyone yet.

## Findings, worst first

1. **Online has no server.** `project.godot` has `network/server_url=""`; the Pages workflow only stamps one in if the
   `SERVER_URL` variable exists. So on the live link, Online says "isn't available yet", and Hotspot needs the Android
   app. **The iPhone web build can play Practice and nothing else.** All multiplayer code (rooms, snapshots,
   prediction, reconnect) is tested headlessly but has never been played by two people on the live link. Fix is yours,
   about ten minutes from a phone: deploy `server/Dockerfile` on a free host per `server/README.md`, then add the
   address as the repo variable `SERVER_URL`. After that I can check online end to end with you.
2. **Practice has nothing that fights back.** The three dummies stand, sway or walk out of the box; they never attack
   (`DummyBrain`). So alone you can never be hit, tagged, or pressed on a capture: the glow, the breath, the capture
   bar and the rescue marker of rounds 13 and 14 can only be seen through the match menu's test buttons. It also
   means **the weapons have never been balanced by playing**: D6 (one Block weapon), D10 (a loadout with no damage),
   D14 and D15 (Heal and Lupa numbers I chose) are all still open. A bot that chases and casts (same `PlayerInput`, same
   sim, deterministic, so testable) would fix the solo experience, and bots against bots headless give win rates per
   weapon, the first balance data this project has. It is a design change to Practice ("dummies don't fight back" is
   in the GDD), so it needs your yes.
3. **Android release is not started.** The workflow builds a *debug* APK; no signing, no AAB, no M6 budget
   measurement (60 fps on a recommended phone, APK under 150 MB, web under 50 MB). The web size and a debug APK size
   can be measured now without a phone; a signed release needs a keystore you create once and store as a secret.
4. **Known broken art.** Toni, Popoy and Inday's meshes are torn (11 953, 12 966 and 15 517 open edges on 9 000
   triangles; the test is "pending" and says so every run). They need your rebuilt rigs from Blender (the asset
   rule: I advise, you make).
5. **New feedback is silent.** Sounds are all code-synthesised and cover casts, hits, tags, rescues and points. The
   things added in rounds 13 and 14 have none: the ready swell, the low-health breath, the capture countdown. Cheap to
   add (no files, nothing to license) and it is half of "feel".
6. **Warnings and debt.** The headless run prints `Parameter "material" is null` and an ObjectDB leak at exit on every
   run. Harmless today; worth one cleanup so a real warning is not lost in them.
7. **Smaller design leftovers.** Online rematch (needs a server change), pings (needs networking), the tag spot for the
   minimap ring (needs a sim event), Filipino weapon blurbs, the native share sheet, and whether to drop the last-match
   strip on 16:9.

you can reach, a phone you have held it on, and an opponent.
